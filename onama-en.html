Our story

Tradition and
innovation since 1973.

Vodolim is a construction company headquartered in Donja Stubica. In the early 1970s the idea emerged to open an independent craft workshop. Vodolim was founded in 1973 by Leopold Brundula. The first workshop, with some basic tools, was opened in the yard of the Brundula family home. The workshop's core activity was water-supply and plumbing installations together with sheet-metal work in construction.

In the mid-1980s Vodolim underwent significant changes: Krešo Brundula took over the business and gave young vocational-school students the opportunity to master their trade. At the beginning of the 1990s Vodolim had the chance to collaborate on larger projects, which led to Katarina Brundula joining the company and driving even greater development, turning it into a respected economic entity in the region. In 2001 the new Vodolim headquarters was completed and put into operation. At that time the company had around fifteen employees. In 2002 a retail and wholesale shop for installation materials and equipment from renowned manufacturers was opened.

At the end of 2014 the third generation, Vanja Brundula, took over. Today Vodolim employs 120 workers and 14 engineers of various specialisations and is positioned among the top 5 installation companies in Croatia. Vodolim's greatest asset is its employees — machines only help; it is people who create new value and who have built the competitive advantage accumulated over decades.

The scope of operations has expanded significantly and Vodolim now operates in two markets. Employees, suppliers, customers and other partners currently come from more than 15 different countries, strengthening business flexibility. Vodolim's core business is water-supply, central-heating, air-conditioning and ventilation installations. Construction works are a second branch in significant growth. The third branch is engineering, allowing Vodolim to offer a complete project-management and construction service — from excavation and structural works through fit-out to handover. The fourth branch is residential development. On the German market, where Vodolim has been present since 2018, it carries out exclusively installation works. Vodolim currently has 150 highly qualified staff.
