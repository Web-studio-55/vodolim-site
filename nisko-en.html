Why a low-energy house?

Vodolim's low-energy house project

In the construction of low-energy houses we use the highest quality materials. Building such a house requires a team of experts consisting of architects and engineers with many years of construction experience.

In Donja Stubica the first true low-energy house was commissioned, using free geothermal heat pump energy from the ground as a heating system, and free solar energy for heating sanitary water. Building such a structure is 35% more expensive than conventional construction, but that difference is quickly offset by enormous savings in energy consumption.

Example project in Donja Stubica

Detached family house with a floor area of 150 m², low-energy "A" energy class — 25 kWh/m²

Heating system: heat pump VIESSMANN VITOCAL 242-G

Solar system: VIESSMANN VITOSOL 200-F

Primary source: 750 m² of land / 500 m of pipe / depth 1.3 m

Secondary: underfloor heating / 1,200 m of pipes

Construction took just over 12 months

Why choose low-energy construction?

Lower utility bills
Monthly utility bills reduced by 80%. The investment pays back in 8–10 years and after that it's pure savings every year.
Earth energy
78% utilisation of earth energy — the geothermal heat pump uses a free natural energy source.
Minimal electricity
Only 22% electricity consumption — solely to run the heat pump compressor.
Solar energy
70% utilisation of solar energy for heating sanitary water — solar collectors as a free heat source.
Return on investment
Return on investment in just 8–10 years! After that, pure energy cost savings every year.
Environmental protection
Save on bills and care for the environment. Low-energy construction reduces CO₂ emissions by more than 70%.
