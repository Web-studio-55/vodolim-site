NPOO.C1.1.2.R3-12.01-V15.0333

Increasing competitiveness through investment in a software solution for Vodolim instalacije d.o.o.

SHORT PROJECT DESCRIPTION

The project consists of two main components:

1

Procurement and implementation of digital tools

2

Strengthening employees' digital skills

The project will ensure a significant increase in the competitiveness of VODOLIM INSTALACIJE d.o.o. through the procurement and implementation of new digital solutions for digital business transformation and strengthening employees' digital skills (training), resulting in significant improvements to organisational practices and a large number of business processes.

The investment will ensure significant optimisation of operations at both internal and external levels and rationalisation of resources, which will also be reflected in overall business profitability.

The project will contribute to:

Increased productivity

Increased number of clients

Increased revenue

Digitalisation of business processes
